Looping

This video piece was made with the idea of it looping when it replayed. For this, I decided to go with putting mascara on. At the time of making this piece, mascara was something that I associated with beauty and emphaszing my eyes. Referencing this in this piece makes the video feel like I am coming back over and over to put the mascara on or reapply.
