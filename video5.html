Audio Replacement Video

This is a video piece I made with the idea of replacing the original audio of the video with new audio to match. I used a variaty of items to create the sounds such as leaves, crinkled paper, tapping and sctratching wood, as well as playing with sheets and blankets. The project reminde me of ASMR (autonomous sensory meridian response) because of how close and intimate the sounds are.
