Meme Mashup

In this project, I mashed up multiple memes together that orignially had different meanings so that when they are brought together, they are one meaning. Prior to making this piece, I had little to no experience using Photoshop. After making the piece however, I feel like I have a better understanding of what the application has to offer and I have continued practicing using it since.
