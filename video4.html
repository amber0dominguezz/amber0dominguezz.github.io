Relfection

This video piece was made with the idea that I have two seperate lives. One from back in my hometown Santa Maria, where agriculture,farms, and old memories is all there is. Two in San Jose, where I am academic, aesthetic, and learning how to be an adult. Exploring these two sides of me in this video has allowed me to embrace them a lot more. Before I would always try to connect them into one person, but personally I am unable and I let them shine when I am at their respective locations.
