Artist Statement

Ever since I was a kid, I have been obsessed with video games and how different they all are. Growing up with game franchises like The Walking Dead, Uncharted, and Legend of Zelda, they inspired me to explore the interaction between a game and its player.

I am storyteller wanting to put my abilities into creating interactive and puzzle games. My goal is to have a library of games that I fully made myself that show off my skills enough to work alongside the companies NaughtyDog, Nintendo, or Epic Games.
